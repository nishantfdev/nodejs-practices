🌿 Prajnaa Farm Kalijeeri – Bitter Cumin for Deep Detox & Digestive Strength
Ancient Wisdom. Mountain-Grown. Naturally Potent.
Kalijeeri, also known as Bitter Cumin, is the seed of Centratherum Anthelminticum—a revered herb in Ayurvedic tradition. Sourced from the Himalayan foothills, our Kalijeeri is cultivated without chemicals and harvested with care by local farmers who honor its medicinal legacy.
🌱 Key Benefits
- Gut Cleanser & Dewormer: Traditionally used to eliminate intestinal parasites like roundworms and tapeworms.
- Blood Purifier: Supports detoxification and toxin elimination from the bloodstream.
- Skin & Hair Support: Helps reduce itching, irritation, and promotes scalp circulation for hair growth.
- Metabolic Balance: Aids in regulating post-meal blood sugar spikes and improving digestion.
🧪 Ayurvedic Profile
- Rasa (Taste): Bitter, Pungent
- Virya (Potency): Hot
- Guna (Quality): Light, Sharp
- Effect on Doshas: Balances Kapha and Vata
🍵 How to Use
Traditionally consumed with Ajwain and Methi for weight management and digestive health. Can be powdered and taken with warm water or infused into herbal blends. Due to its intense bitterness, start with small doses under guidance.
